# Public-Source Boundaries

## Purpose

Ensure public artifacts are created from original work, public evidence, and
synthetic examples.

## Procedure

1. Confirm every source is approved and publicly accessible.
2. Record provenance and license compatibility when adapting material.
3. Replace real examples with neutral synthetic examples.
4. Exclude private names, repositories, notes, recordings, transcripts, URLs,
   identifiers, and operational details.
5. Stop when provenance or ownership cannot be established.

## Boundaries

Concept-level metadata may cross into public planning. Private source content
may not.

## Failure and escalation

Leave uncertain material out and escalate ownership or licensing ambiguity.

## Validation

Run the repository public-safety validator and complete a human diff review.
