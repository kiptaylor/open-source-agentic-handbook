# Evidence and Validation

## Purpose

Connect conclusions and completed work to inspectable evidence and acceptance
criteria.

## Procedure

1. Identify the evidence required by the task envelope.
2. Separate facts, inferences, proposals, and unresolved uncertainty.
3. Run proportionate automated and human checks.
4. Record failures and the artifact revision that was tested.
5. Accept only when every required criterion is supported.

## Boundaries

Absence of evidence is not evidence of success. Validation cannot waive policy
or expand scope.

## Failure and escalation

Reject unsupported results and escalate when the required check cannot be run.

## Validation

Use an independent verifier for consequential or publication-bound artifacts.
