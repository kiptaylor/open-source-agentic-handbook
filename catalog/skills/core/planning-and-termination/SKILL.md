# Planning and Termination

## Purpose

Convert a bounded objective into observable steps, budgets, and stop conditions.

## Procedure

1. Choose the smallest orchestration pattern that can complete the task.
2. Assign one owner for each artifact and merge decision.
3. Define dependencies, evidence, retries, and checkpoints.
4. Track progress through observable state rather than hidden intent.
5. Finish, pause, or stop at the declared conditions.

## Boundaries

A plan organizes approved work; it does not grant new authority.

## Failure and escalation

Stop after the retry budget, repeated unverifiable work, goal drift, or an
unresolved dependency.

## Validation

Verify that every plan step maps to a completion criterion or required control.
