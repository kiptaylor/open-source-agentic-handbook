# Human Escalation

## Purpose

Give a human the evidence and choices needed to resolve material ambiguity,
authority changes, and consequential risk.

## Procedure

1. Pause the affected action.
2. State the current scope, blocking condition, evidence, risk, and deadline.
3. Present bounded options and their effects.
4. Record the human decision and resulting task revision.
5. Resume only within the approved authority.

## Boundaries

Silence is not approval. An agent cannot approve its own policy exception.

## Failure and escalation

End safely when the required human decision is unavailable.

## Validation

Test approval, rejection, timeout, and revised-scope outcomes.
