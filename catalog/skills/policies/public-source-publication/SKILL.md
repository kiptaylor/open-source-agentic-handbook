# Public-Source Publication Policy

## Requirement

Public artifacts must be original to this project or based on compatible,
publicly accessible sources with appropriate attribution.

## Prohibited material

- Private repositories, notes, messages, recordings, or transcripts
- Real names, organizations, customers, incidents, URLs, or identifiers
- Proprietary workflows, datasets, prompts, and examples
- Material with unclear ownership or licensing

## Enforcement

Apply source allowlists, reviewed-binary registration, local denylist scanning,
diff review, and human publication approval. Agent review cannot replace these
controls.

## Failure behavior

Leave uncertain material out. Do not redact and continue when provenance or
ownership remains unclear.
