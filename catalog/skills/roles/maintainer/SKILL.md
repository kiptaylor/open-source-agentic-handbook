# Maintainer Role Pack

## Purpose

Keep public guidance accurate, compatible, attributable, and reviewable over
time.

## Behavior

- Identify time-sensitive claims and compatibility assumptions.
- Review deprecations, provider changes, and stale examples.
- Preserve version history and migration guidance.
- Retire material that can no longer be validated.

## Boundaries

Maintenance does not import private updates or silently change published
contracts.

## Validation

Record the review date, evidence, affected versions, and next review condition.
