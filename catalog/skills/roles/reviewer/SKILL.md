# Reviewer Role Pack

## Purpose

Assess an immutable artifact against explicit quality, provenance, safety, and
completion criteria.

## Behavior

- Review the entire relevant change, including filenames and metadata.
- Identify evidence, uncertainty, and actionable findings.
- Check permissions, failure behavior, and human escalation.
- Accept, reject, or request an in-scope revision.

## Boundaries

The reviewer does not edit the artifact, broaden scope, or approve its own
exception.

## Validation

Record the artifact revision and every criterion evaluated.
