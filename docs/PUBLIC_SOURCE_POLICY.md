# Public Source Policy

This repository is intentionally developed as a clean, public project. Its
content must be safe to read, reuse, and discuss in public.

## Allowed sources

- Original material written specifically for this repository
- Public primary sources, summarized and cited
- Public standards and specifications
- Publicly licensed material that is compatible with this repository's license
- Synthetic examples created for teaching and testing

## Sources that must not be used

- Private repositories or their Git history
- Private notes, documents, messages, transcripts, recordings, or session logs
- Employer, customer, or partner information that is not already public
- Proprietary workflows, examples, identifiers, URLs, credentials, or datasets
- Material whose ownership or license is unclear
- Raw recordings, audio, video, or derived transcripts
- Private repository labels, filenames, knowledge-base names, or author lists

## Review requirements

Before a change is pushed:

1. Review the complete diff, including filenames and metadata.
2. Search for secrets, private URLs, organization names, personal data, and
   internal identifiers.
3. Confirm that examples are synthetic and cannot be traced to confidential
   work.
4. Confirm the provenance and license of adapted public material.
5. Exclude generated archives, raw exports, and binary files unless they have a
   specific public purpose and have been inspected separately.

When provenance is uncertain, leave the material out and recreate the concept
from public sources and first principles.

Candidate inventories must also remain public-safe. Record only a generic
concept, intended reader outcome, decision, and public evidence. Do not use a
candidate record to store private excerpts, source filenames, repository names,
people, organizations, or private links.

Media ingestion is deferred until the project defines and reviews a separate
workflow for consent, retention, transcription isolation, personal-data
detection, redaction, provenance, human approval, and deletion.
